# Start here

This is a test vault for the Kiri Focus Timer plugin. It holds sample notes only.

1. Click the timer icon in the left ribbon, or run the command "Kiri Focus Timer: Open Kiri".
2. Open a note in `Projects` to see its open tasks in the Kiri panel.
3. Turn on "All notes" to see tasks from every note.
4. For a quick test, type `5` in the custom minutes box. Or set a break length of 1 minute in settings.
5. When a block ends, look in today's note in `Daily` under "Focus log".

Settings to try: "Count blocks on the task" and "Ask to mark the task done".

## New features

Open [[New features]] for sample tasks. In short:

1. **Focus on this task.** Right-click an open task in any note and choose "Focus on this task". The task is picked and a block starts.
2. **Focus stats.** Click "Focus stats" in the Kiri panel, or run "Show focus stats". `Daily` has log lines for 13 and 14 November, so the streak and "Time per note" have data.
3. **Long breaks.** In settings, under Breaks, set "Long break every" to 2. Run two 5 minute blocks: the panel shows "Focus · 1 of 2", then "Focus · 2 of 2", then a long break.
4. **Start the break yourself.** Turn off "Start the break by itself". When a block ends, the panel shows "Start break" and "Start next block".
5. **Soft chime.** In settings, set "Chime sound" to soft and click the play button next to it.
6. **Time spent.** Turn on "Add time spent to the task" (under Tasks). Pick "Write the product page" in [[New features]] and finish a block: the line gets `[spent:: 5m]` and the panel shows "Spent 5m of 1h planned".
7. **Commands.** Settings → Hotkeys, search "Kiri": Start or pause, Skip break, Stop timer and the others have no hotkeys until you add one.

## Kiri account (not live yet)

The server side of the account connection is not live yet, so Connect will show an error for now. When it is live:

1. Settings → Kiri Focus Timer → Kiri account → Connect. A page opens in your browser and the settings show a code.
2. Check the code on the page, then click Allow. The settings show "Connected as ..." and your plan.
3. The sound list in the panel now shows the Kiri sounds. Locked ones say "(locked)" and link to the pricing page. The first play downloads the sound and shows "Loading ..." under the list.
4. If your plan includes them, "Binaural beats" appears under Sound in settings. Off by default.
5. Finish a block: it appears in your history on project-kiri.com.
6. Disconnect brings back the local noise and deletes the downloaded sounds.
